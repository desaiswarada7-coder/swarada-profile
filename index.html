I'm someone who likes asking questions. Especially about why things work the way they do, why they sometimes don't, and how those failures show up in people's everyday lives.

I'm fascinated by public systems: the policies, institutions, and processes that shape people's everyday lives. I'm interested in research and data, but I'm not someone who wants to understand the world entirely through a spreadsheet. I really enjoy talking to people, spending time on the ground, and uncovering problems that aren't immediately obvious.

Having worked across primary data collection, academic research, data analysis using software, and program-specific monitoring and evaluations, I currently lead Research and Development at Upekkha Foundation.

Ultimately, I am drawn to creating collaborative, practical solutions to everyday problems.

Ideas & Explorations

Problems I've been thinking about, ideas I've been developing, and possibilities I'd like to explore further.

Social enterprise idea

SwachhBridge

A collaborative approach to urban sanitation that connects NGOs, citizen volunteers and municipal bodies to coordinate cleanliness initiatives and mobilise existing civic resources. Goes beyond clean-up drives by exploring research-backed waste prevention mechanisms and public reporting systems to improve accountability and sustain cleaner public spaces.

Research & advocacy idea

Women's Safety & Freedom of Movement

An initiative exploring how public infrastructure and the design of urban spaces shape women's safety and freedom of movement in Pune. Through research, comparative case studies, expert perspectives and public engagement, it aims to understand how infrastructure such as street lighting, footpaths, public transport facilities and public amenities affects women's ability to access and navigate the city, and to advance safer, more inclusive urban environments.

Governance & technology product idea

Public Partnership Mapping

A proposed digital platform to map and track government collaborations with NGOs, CSR initiatives and other development partners, beginning with Pune's public education system. By bringing together information on geographical coverage, funding arrangements, project deliverables and timelines, the platform aims to help government identify overlaps, address gaps and make more informed decisions about future partnerships.

Social enterprise / business model idea

Development Partnership Exchange

A proposed social enterprise that bridges the gap between government development priorities, NGO implementation capacity and corporate CSR funding. Starting at the district level, the venture would map local development needs, identify suitable NGO partners and align them with CSR funding opportunities. It would facilitate project development, institutional coordination, funding partnerships and implementation support to enable government-prioritised development projects to be delivered efficiently and sustainably.
